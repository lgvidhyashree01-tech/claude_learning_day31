How to Play

Your Role

You are the Head of Operations for a global supply chain company. Your job is to resolve incoming alerts and keep KPIs as high as possible within 3 minutes.

Alert Priority

  • 🔴 Critical — Act immediately; KPI damage starts at once
  • 🟠 High — Resolve within seconds to avoid escalation
  • 🔵 Medium — Important but manageable
  • 🟢 Low — Resolve when possible

Actions

  • Each alert has 3–4 action buttons
  • Actions differ in cost, speed, and effectiveness
  • Best actions → improve KPIs and add score
  • "Ignore" is always risky — use carefully

KPIs to Protect

  • Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health
  • Transportation Efficiency, Operating Cost, Revenue Protected

Scoring

Best action = +150 pts. Good action = +80 pts. Ignore/bad = −100 pts. Resolving quickly adds a time bonus.
